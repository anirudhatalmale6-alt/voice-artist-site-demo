About

Jarred Thornhill is an American voice actor based in St. Louis, Missouri.

He came to it as a fan. Years of watching anime and playing video games, then convention after convention where the voices behind those characters turned out to be people you could actually stand and talk to. Meeting them is what moved it from something he loved to something he wanted to do.

He is building his reel now, starting with the commercial demo above. Scripts, auditions and enquiries are all welcome.
